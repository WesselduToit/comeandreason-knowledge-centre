Heavenly Records: rap sheet or medical chart?

The Bible speaks of books in heaven, of names written and blotted out, of the dead judged "as recorded in the books". Read through imposed law those books are a prosecutor's file. Read through design law they are a physician's chart. This page puts the same verses through both readings and asks which one lets every text stand.
